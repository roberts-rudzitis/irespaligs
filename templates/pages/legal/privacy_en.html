Effective from [DATE]. In case of differences, the Latvian version prevails.

1. Who we are

Controller: [COMPANY NAME], reg. no. [NUMBER], registered address [ADDRESS]. Data protection contact: {{ support_email }}.

2. What data we process

  • Your account: email address, name (if given), language, payment details you add for notices, a password hash (never the password itself).
  • Data you enter about your properties and tenants: property names and addresses, tenants' name, email and phone, lease terms, meter readings, payment notices and payments.

3. Why and on what basis

  • To provide the service and perform our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To meet invoicing and accounting obligations (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • For security and improving the service (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Tenant data

You (the landlord) are the controller of the tenant data you enter. We process it on your behalf as a processor, under the "Data processing agreement" section of the Terms.

5. Where data is stored and who receives it

All data is stored in the European Union. We use these processors:

  • Render (hosting and database, Frankfurt, Germany)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • Brevo (sending emails, France)

6. How long we keep data

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, all data you entered is deleted immediately. Accounting records of your subscription payments are kept as long as the law requires ([PERIOD]).

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and receive it in a portable format. Under "My account" you can download all your data or delete your account at any time. You may lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (www.dvi.gov.lv).

8. Cookies

We only use necessary cookies: for your login session, security (CSRF), language choice and remembering this notice. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

9. Changes

We will inform you by email about significant changes.
